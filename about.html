About

I build products, and run a studio that keeps them sharp.

I'm Tanish, a digital product designer and the founder of Wasabie Studio. I design and ship my own products, and run the studio doing the same work for clients. The studio is the commercial engine; the products are where the real bets live.

Design and code aren't separate steps for me. I spec tightly, prototype in the browser, and handle the edge cases before anything ships. Trend-chasing comes last, if at all. The studio is proof the work holds up under real clients and real deadlines; the products are where I find out what I'd build with no brief but my own.

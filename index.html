03 / About
Background & direction

I build products, then run a studio to fund and sharpen how I build. Wasabie is the commercial engine; the products are where the real bets live.

Design and code aren't separate steps for me. I specify tightly, prototype in the browser, and cover the edge cases before anything ships. Trend-chasing comes last, if at all.

Right now I'm building toward products that stand on their own, with the studio as proof the work holds up under real clients and real deadlines.

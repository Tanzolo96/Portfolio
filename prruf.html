01 · The premise

What if remembering your own work cost you nothing?

I kept forgetting to log my work. So we built something that quietly notices what you’re doing, privately, on your own machine, and keeps the notes for you.

Not a boss’s surveillance tool. A note-taker that works for the person being recorded. Built with Thishya, my co-founder, who was facing the exact same problem.

02 · The story

A small, boring problem I refused to leave alone

In an internship I was asked to keep a progress log. I made it easy: dictate the task to Claude, let it write the entry. It lasted two weeks. Then I quit.

Everyone’s “record” is scattered: a note here, an email there, a Notion doc, a text to yourself. Nobody keeps it. It’s a chore stacked on the work.

It came back once I was running Wasabie and Thishya mentioned she was facing the exact same thing - hearing it from her validated it for both of us. So we asked one thing: what if the laptop just saw what I was doing? The smallest test answered it - it felt like someone sitting beside me, quietly taking notes.

“When we showed real client-work people, the feedback wasn’t about the concept. It was fear. Every safeguard came out of that.”

06 · Not surveillance

Most tools here are built for the wrong person

The Hubstaffs and Time Doctors are employee-monitoring software: the employer installs them, the data flows up to a manager, and the person on the keyboard is the subject. Prruf is the opposite, on purpose.

Surveillance toolsPrruf
Installed by the employerInstalled by you, for you
Data flows up to a managerThe record is yours - you decide what's sent
Stores screenshots & keystrokesOnly text. The image is deleted the instant it's read.
Scores and ranks youNo scores. It just describes.
You're the subjectYou're the owner

It’s not a marketing angle. The safeguards are baked into the code as rules we said we’d never break:

prruf · privacy rules
< privacy rules >
screenshot→ memory only (never written to disk)
image→ deleted immediately after read
stored→ text only, always
data flows→ nowhere. you own the record.
shared→ only what you choose to share

08 · Why Prruf

Remembering your own work should cost you nothing

Everyone deserves a clean record of what they did, without a timer to start, a log to write, or a boss watching over their shoulder. That’s the whole reason Prruf exists: the record is yours, and no one has to be trusted for it.

“What if remembering your own work cost you nothing, and no one had to be trusted for it?”

It’s a working app for Mac, currently in testing. Studios and freelancers are using it a week at a time, and their feedback is deciding what we build next.

Built: capture, task grouping, break detection and one-click reports.
